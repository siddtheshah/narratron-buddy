A few ways to begin

Mix these recipes freely—each is a starting spark, not a script.

Tabletop campaign

The map that remembers

Use a hand-drawn map and location references as your visual anchor. Let viewers annotate a route, then turn on collaboration mode to vote for the next dangerous detour. Bring the experience into your game with the Narratron Foundry module.

Try: custom map + Drifting haze + audience suggestions

Listening party

Album as a doorway

Build a visual story around an original track or playlist. Use image uploads for the artist's moodboard, then pass the baton between hosts as each song opens a new chapter. Let AI music bridge the quiet moments between tracks.

Try: uploaded music + AI fill + custom style + shared baton

Classroom story lab

The expedition log

Start with archival photos, student artwork, or a field-trip image. Give the class a mission and let their collective suggestions steer the narrator through the next discovery.

Try: image uploads + Light trace + collaboration mode

Cozy mystery night

The last lantern at Briar House

Set a moody playlist beneath a manor portrait and faded clues. Hand the baton to the player whose character is questioned, then let the room vote on which lead to chase. AI music keeps the tension alive when your playlist runs dry.

Try: uploaded music + AI fill + Creeping darkness + baton pass

Podcast visualizer

Make the episode visible

Give a podcast episode its own visual language. Upload guest photos, archival images, or a moodboard, then let Narratron create a shifting companion world for the conversation.

Try: uploaded audio + image references + custom style

Comedy night

The presentation nobody asked for

Turn a silly premise into a dead-serious visual presentation: explain a pet’s secret career, defend an absurd invention, or reveal the history of an imaginary town. Pass the baton so every presenter gets their moment.

Try: custom images + Bendy + shared baton
