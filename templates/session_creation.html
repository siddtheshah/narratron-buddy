📚 Deployment Architecture

Each deployment creates an isolated workspace under sessions/<session_id>/ containing:

  • session.json: Workspace metadata & settings
  • reference_library/: Reference files for Gemini image generation
  • playlists/: Multi-folder audio playlists
  • output/: Generated images and narrative logs

🔑 Each session automatically receives a unique Join Key. Owners can copy and share the key to allow viewers to join from the /join splash screen.
