Orator Quick Start Guide

How to interact with Narratron as the Orator
1
Connect & Stream Microphone 🔌 Connect Agent
Click Connect Agent (or press hotkey Ctrl+Shift+[) to connect your canvas theater to the Narratron AI agent and start real-time microphone streaming.
2
Interact & Speak Live 🔌 Connect ➔ 🎙️ Disconnect
Speak to the agent in real time while using the bottom toolbar to doodle on canvas. Press the hotkey again (or click Disconnect) to end the session.
💡
Tips for Orators
• Use headphones to prevent background audio echo.
• Use the bottom toolbar to doodle live on canvas.
• Open pullout menu ➔ Hotkey to customize your shortcut.
