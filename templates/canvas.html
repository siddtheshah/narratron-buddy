Orator Quick Start Guide

How to interact with Narratron as the Orator
1
Enable Microphone Input 🎙️ Mic Control
When the agent is running and this tab is active, the canvas connects automatically. Click Enable Mic (or press hotkey Ctrl+Shift+[) to begin real-time microphone streaming, then click Mute Mic to stop it.
2
Interact & Speak Live 🔌 Connect ➔ 🎙️ Disconnect
Speak to the agent in real time while using the bottom toolbar to doodle on canvas. Press the hotkey again (or click Disconnect) to end the session.
3
Command Narratron via Text 💬 Text Input
Prefer typing or directing the agent quietly? Press hotkey Shift+Space to summon the command prompt and send instructions directly to Narratron.
💡
Tips for Orators
• Use headphones to prevent background audio echo.
• Use the bottom toolbar to doodle live on canvas.
• Press Shift+Space to type a direct command to Narratron without speaking.
• Open ✨ menu ➔ Input Configuration to customize your mic toggle and text input shortcuts.
✨

Collaborate with Narratron

Your ideas can help shape the next moment.
1
Doodle on the canvas ✏️ Draw
Use the colors and brush controls in the bottom bar to point, circle, sketch, or add a visual twist to the scene.
2
Offer a suggestion 💡 /suggest
Type /suggest your idea in chat. Other viewers can give it a +1; the top idea is selected first.
3
Watch for the moment
You can suggest, vote, and doodle anytime. When the orator enables collaboration, Narratron receives the leading suggestion and the annotated canvas.

Adventure Mode Guide

How to immerse yourself in Narratron's crafted narratives
1
Immerse in a Crafted Narrative Story Mode
Adventure mode is built around a pre-existing sandbox and structure. Make meaningful choices in a story that is never told the same way twice.
2
Play In-Character, Don't Force Control
Speak your character's dialogue and describe your actions. If you have your own story to show or tell, it is better to disable adventure mode and take full control.
3
Dynamic Dialogue & Reactions
NPC interactions appear in live canvas dialogue, while illustrative scenes and soundtrack dynamically respond to your choices.
4
Audience Collaboration
Viewers in chat can brainstorm tactical ideas and plot moves to help guide your character through challenges.
💡
Tips for Adventure Mode
• Treat the world as real! React to situations, investigate clues, and let the narrator reveal consequences.
• Re-open this guide anytime from the left pullout menu.
