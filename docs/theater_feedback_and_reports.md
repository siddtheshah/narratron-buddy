# Feedback and Reporting

1. Sign in and open the theater you want to report.
2. Open the left-hand menu and select **Feedback/Report Theater**.
3. Choose a **Report type** and describe what happened.
4. Select **Send Report** and wait for confirmation.

For malicious activity, a snapshot of the theater is included for review.
You can also use this form to report a bug or suggest an improvement. For bugs,
include the steps to reproduce the problem and what you expected to happen.
