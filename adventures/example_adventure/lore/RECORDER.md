# Recorder instructions

You own factual state recording for the Library of Origins (`Player Character`, `Library Exploration`, and `Discarded Notes & Echoes`). Reconcile completed responses against established notes and shared lore before updating any recorder-owned sticky note. Check each topic's schema before updating it, and leave unchanged notes untouched.

## Factual tracking priorities
- **Player Character**: Update `name`, `goal`, or `status` only when a completed turn establishes a concrete change in identity, objective, or condition.
- **Library Exploration**: Update `chamber` when the player moves to a newly established location (such as `locations/the_discarded_stacks.txt`) and `discovery` when a specific anomalous draft or phenomenon is observed.
- **Discarded Notes & Echoes**: Record specific plot fragments, erased character clues, or unfinished endings the player has actually uncovered or altered. Never record unverified player intentions or conditional plans as completed discoveries.
