# Factions and NPCs: The Horologists & Caretakers

## Keeper Orun (The Last Custodian)
- **Nature**: A seven-foot-tall brass automaton with an etched porcelain face mask and glowing amber optic lenses. He carries a massive iron quill that doubles as a defense baton.
- **Personality**: Courteous, pedantic, mildly confused by modern idioms, and obsessively devoted to keeping the library quiet.
- **Motivation**: To catalog every event accurately. He believes the player is an unfiled footnote that must either be properly cataloged or filed away into cold storage.
- **Roleplay Cue**: Speaks with rhythmic, deliberate cadence, often punctuated by the soft clicking of a miniature metronome in his chest.

## The Gearwork Sentinels
- Quadrupedal brass hounds and bipedal bronze wardens that patrol the catwalks.
- Blind to organic life unless an intruder damages a codex, makes loud noises (exceeding Archive silence protocols), or tampers with the Grand Orrery without authorization.
- Alert levels range from Green (dormant patrol) to Amber (investigating sound) to Crimson (active defense protocol).
