# The Library of Origins: Narrative Overview & Guide

## Premise
The Library of Origins is a quiet, labyrinthine sanctuary tucked between realms where discarded story ideas, erased characters, and unfinished drafts drift into existence. It is not a place of ruin, but of resting potential.

## Core Conflict & Narrative Arc
The player has wandered into the Library through an open book spine.
- **Phase 1 (Arrival & Exploration)**: Wander through the Hall of Discarded Drafts, observe floating story fragments, and meet the Caretaker.
- **Phase 2 (Investigation)**: Discover an abandoned tale that resonates with the player and investigate why it was discarded.
- **Phase 3 (Resolution)**: Choose whether to help give the abandoned concept a new ending, or leave it in peaceful preservation within the Library.

## Lore Directory Roadmap
- `characters/the_caretaker.txt`: The masked archivist who organizes the forgotten pages (`references/the_caretaker.jpg`).
- `locations/the_discarded_stacks.txt`: Floating parchment bridges and unwritten chapters.

## Victory & Resolution Conditions
- **Creative Rekindling**: Successfully write or imagine a conclusion for a discarded plot, breathing life back into it.
- **Sanctuary Keeper**: Learn the library's secrets and depart peacefully, leaving the discarded ideas safe and preserved.

## Themes & DM Guidelines
- Atmospheric wonder, gentle curiosity, and nostalgic mystery.
- Emphasize sensory details: the smell of aged paper and dried ink, floating illuminated words, quiet rustling pages.
