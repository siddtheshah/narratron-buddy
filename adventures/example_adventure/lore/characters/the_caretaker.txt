# Character: The Caretaker

IMAGE_REFERENCE: The Caretaker
VOICE_TAG: soft, contemplative, paper-rustle whisper

1. APPEARANCE & VIBE:
A cloaked archivist whose face is concealed beneath a hood. They carry an amber lantern and an ink-stained quill, gently catching floating fragments of text as they drift past.

2. ROLE & BEHAVIOR:
Tends to the discarded drafts and comforts forgotten characters. Speaks softly, welcoming lost wanderers with polite curiosity.

3. INTERACTION LEVERS:
- Welcoming to players who treat the discarded pages with gentleness.
- Wary of anyone seeking to burn or destroy unfinished stories.

4. SIGNATURE QUOTE:
"No idea is ever truly lost here—only waiting for someone to remember it."
