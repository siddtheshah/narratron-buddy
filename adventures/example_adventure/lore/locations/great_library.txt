# Locations: The Great Library & Stacks

## The Grand Vestibule
- Entry chamber with soaring vaulted ceilings made of polished granite and interlocking brass arches.
- A massive pendular pendulum swings across the floor, tracing intricate geometric lines in fine copper sand.
- A sealed archway leads north into the Stacks; opening it requires turning three interlocking wall dials to the constellation of the Weaver.

## The Stacks of Infinity
- Multi-tiered iron spiral staircases and moving brass bookshelves that slide along ceiling rails with deep metallic thrums.
- Thousands of metallic scroll-tubes contain thin sheets of engraved gold foil recording historical chronicles.
- Hazard: Careless footsteps can trigger shifting book-stacks, temporarily rearranging corridors.

## The Grand Orrery Chamber
- The heart of the Archive. A hollow spherical chamber containing floating brass rings representing celestial bodies, powered by a central radiant core.
- The Heart of Gears is encased within the central astrolabe, protected by three rotating cipher locks.
