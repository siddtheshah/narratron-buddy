# Location: The Discarded Stacks

1. OVERVIEW:
Soaring vaulted arches lined with towering mahogany shelves that stretch into misty heights. Thousands of parchment pages float softly on air currents like glowing leaves.

2. KEY AREAS:
- **The Hall of Discarded Drafts**: The entryway where rough outlines and deleted dialogue scurry along the floorboards like mice.
- **The Marginalia Gallery**: A quiet balcony overlooking a sea of floating inkwells and forgotten character sketches.
- **The Desk of Origins**: An ancient cedar drafting table where visitors can pen their own additions or read unfinished epilogues.
