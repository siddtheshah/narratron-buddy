predicate

Evaluates a yes/no condition against shared evidence and returns a calibrated probability in [0.0, 1.0].

choice

Selects one option from up to 255 discrete choices, returning the winning choice, per-option probabilities, and confidence.

score

Rates evidence on an ordered rubric of levels, returning a probability-weighted continuous score and level distribution.
